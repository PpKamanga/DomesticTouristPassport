About Domestic Tourist Passport

The Domestic Tourist Passport is a tourism engagement platform designed to help tourists explore local attractions, record visits, and earn reward points called Footprints.

The system encourages visitors to discover destinations while also helping tourism administrators understand visitor activity, destination engagement, and tourist feedback.

Tourists can browse attractions, view destination details, record visits, complete feedback surveys, and track their progress through a digital passport experience.

Administrators can review destination activity and use analytics to support better tourism planning, marketing, and visitor experience improvements.
